Open Hiring

A job platform based on the Open Hiring principle: candidates are hired in order of sign-up instead of on CVs or interviews, with a strong focus on connecting local talent with local companies.

My role
Accessibility (WCAG) lead, location feature
Client
IO Digital
Tech
PHP · Laravel · Tailwind · JavaScript
Context
CMGT team project
CMGT Showcase pageView code on GitHub
My role

Making it usable for everyone

I was the team's WCAG / accessibility expert. I ran accessibility tests with fellow students and brought many pages from inaccessible to WCAG AA, some even to AAA. I also built a feature that shows local companies near the user's location.

Platform features

  • Registration and profiles for job seekers and employers.
  • Posting and managing vacancies with location data.
  • First come, first served applications (Open Hiring).
Reflection

What I learned

Deep experience with Laravel and accessibility. Working with IO Digital showed me how a company handles client requirements, documentation and project management, and how big the difference in professionalism is compared to a school project. They also gave me a lot of valuable practical tips.
